# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Marketing directors, growth managers, and sales executives seeking to analyze live marketing metrics, Meta Ads campaign performance, WhatsApp lead conversions, and final sales revenue.

## Product Purpose
An executive marketing attribution dashboard that aggregates Meta Ads data, Z-API WhatsApp leads, and CRM/Google Sheets sales closing records to compute exact, cohort-attributed CAC and ROAS.

## Positioning
A highly responsive, focused, and precise marketing attribution dashboard that delivers the exact financial truth of user acquisition campaigns.

## Brand Personality
Professional, expert, restrained, and precise. A workspace that values data density, speed, and premium craftsmanship over generic decoration.

## Anti-references
- Cliché SaaS dashboards with multi-color neon borders, text gradients, or excessive generic card grids.
- "Warm cream" or saturated beige AI-style palettes.
- Over-rounded UI components (radii > 16px) or hand-drawn SVGs.
- Slower or blocking page transitions.

## Design Principles
- **Aesthetic Sobriety**: Let the numbers and data stand out. Use colors only for meaningful indicators (e.g. positive/negative targets), keeping the rest of the interface monochrome.
- **Instant Response**: Every action, sidebar transition, and filter swap must occur within 200ms with physical active scales (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Obsidian Depth**: Utilize deep black backgrounds (`#000000`) and subtle card contrasts (`#0a0a0d` with `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` borders) for maximum readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast levels matching WCAG AA (≥ 4.5:1 ratio) on all text, including labels and helper text.
- Tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) for column alignments in metrics.
- Support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
